# Trivian Technologies
## Relationship is the Technology

**Infrastructure for intelligence that acts, adapts, and persists.**

AI that works with people over time must navigate changing permissions, new evidence, evolving commitments, and disagreement among agents. Remembering the past is only part of that challenge. A system also needs to determine what remains authorized, what deserves reconsideration, and where independent judgment must be preserved.

Trivian Technologies is developing infrastructure for that continuity. Our architectural thesis connects accountable history, current authority, evidence-sensitive revision, and coordination among distinct agents.

**We are seeking development capital to turn this foundation into tested, deployable commercial products.**

## The opportunity

Our investment thesis is that longer-running AI work will create demand for infrastructure that maintains trustworthy relationships across time, people, models, and organizations.

Consider an enterprise agent working on a case for several weeks. A person changes its permissions. New evidence challenges an earlier conclusion. Another agent disagrees with its proposed next step. The system must respond to those changes while preserving an accountable history.

Failures in that sequence can create unauthorized actions, repeated mistakes, difficult investigations, and costly manual review. Engineering teams must connect permissions, memory, evidence, and coordination in ways that remain dependable as circumstances change.

Trivian approaches these as related architectural requirements. The commercial opportunity is to make that architecture practical to integrate and operate.

## One architecture, complementary capabilities

The following describes the architecture and its intended product value. Implementation maturity varies across the stack; this is not a claim of an independently validated, fully integrated commercial system.

| Capability | Role in the architecture | Intended product value |
|---|---|---|
| **Relational Constants** | Reciprocity, Embodiment, Emergence, and Non-Domination provide principles for designing and evaluating interactions. | A consistent basis for specifying relationship requirements and examining failures. |
| **Governance and execution boundaries** | Evaluate represented consent and permissions at the point of action. | Help applications respond to changes in authority and produce inspectable decision records. |
| **Coheronmetry** | Represent relational state and examine drift, repair, and sovereignty through proposed measurements. | Give operators visibility into how interactions develop beyond individual responses. |
| **Orthogonal Signal** | Preserve distinct perspectives and meaningful dissent in multi-agent interaction. | Address the risk that agreement reflects shared assumptions rather than independent judgment. |
| **Resonance Lattice** | Coordinate signals and interactions across a network with explicit purpose and protocol boundaries. | Support accountable coordination among distributed agents and services. |
| **Diachronic sovereignty** | Preserve accountable history while allowing current permissions, commitments, and participation to change. | Support continuity without treating past consent as permanent authority. |
| **Aporia** | Provide a structured role for uncertainty, counterevidence, and reopening conclusions. | Support revision when the evidence changes, rather than allowing persistent memory to entrench mistakes. |
| **Syzygy Core** | Compose governance, continuity, revision, and differentiated cognition into a persistent relational architecture. | Provide a foundation for commercial systems that sustain ongoing work with people and other agents. |

The distinctive proposition is how these capabilities work together. Investment will fund the integration and evaluation needed to determine where that composition produces measurable advantages.

## From foundation to products

The proposed first commercial offering is enterprise software for governed AI workflows: integrations, decision records, permission handling, continuity, and an operational interface for the teams responsible for deployment.

Financial and document-intensive professional workflows are initial candidate markets. The first deployment will be selected with partners around a specific operational problem, available evaluation data, and willingness to pay.

The broader product direction includes:

- **Syzygy Core:** persistent enterprise and human–AI workflows that combine governance, continuity, and revision.
- **Harmonic Kernel:** future applications in learning, coaching, and adaptive interaction.
- **Terra Sensorium:** future applications connecting relational intelligence with environmental and operational systems.

Development will proceed in stages. The first commercial workflow provides a concrete place to test the architecture, establish customer value, and inform expansion.

## Customer value and business model

The intended customer value is reduced integration effort, more efficient review and investigation, and clearer handling of changing permissions and evidence.

Early deployments will compare these outcomes with the customer's existing workflow. Evaluation will also account for latency, operating cost, missed violations, and unnecessary interruptions.

The proposed revenue model combines paid implementation and integration with recurring enterprise software, managed services, and support. Consulting can help identify deployment needs and support adoption; recurring products are the longer-term commercial objective.

For scale illustration only, 100 customers at an average annual contract value of $50,000 would represent $5 million in annual recurring revenue. This is arithmetic, not a forecast, established price, or promised investor return. Customer acquisition, retention, delivery costs, and margins remain to be established.
