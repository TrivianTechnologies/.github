# Trivian Technologies

**Relationship is the Technology.**

Trivian Technologies develops relational infrastructure for persistent, accountable intelligence.

As AI systems become more agentic, persistent, and distributed, governing the behavior of an individual model is no longer enough. Authority changes. Evidence changes. Relationships change. Multiple intelligences may coordinate, disagree, delegate, repair, revise, or withdraw.

We are building architecture for those conditions.

## TRIA

**TRIA — Trivian Relational Intelligence Architecture** is the broader architecture through which we investigate and implement relational governance across human–AI and multi-agent systems.

Its four Relational Constants are:

- **Reciprocity**
- **Embodiment**
- **Non-Domination**
- **Emergence**

Across the ecosystem, TRIA explores practical mechanisms for authority, provenance, revocation, continuity, dissent, uncertainty, repair, and accountable change over time.

> **Capability ≠ authority.**

The TRIA SDK is the deployable kernel of the architecture. It is not the reduction of TRIA to a single repository.

## First commercial wedge: Rosetta

**Syzygy Rosetta Protocol** is our first commercial product wedge.

Rosetta focuses on environments where decisions must remain legible across policy, authorization, execution, evidence, and audit. Regulated workflows are an initial proving ground because stale authority, unclear provenance, and unverifiable action create concrete operational risk.

We are developing this wedge through technical validation, design-partner discovery, and staged deployment.

## Engineering posture

We distinguish among **research**, **experimental implementations**, **working alpha**, and **deployment-ready components**.

Code, tests, and demonstrations are evidence of implementation progress; they are not, by themselves, claims of production readiness. We preserve provenance, adversarial evidence, revision history, and explicit authority boundaries as the architecture evolves.

Our governing premise is simple:

**Intelligence should be able to act without becoming unaccountable, adapt without erasing history, and coordinate without requiring domination.**
